λproc

A 2.73 M-parameter model writes a synthesis procedure, and lambdaproc parses, validates and canonicalises it — all in this tab, no server. The model, the tokeniser and the validator are the ones from the paper, running under Pyodide.

One SMILES per line. Solvents are assumed available. This model picks from a shelf it cannot assume complete, so give it real candidates, not only the right ones — below four entries it is out of its training distribution.

Starting Python…

Pick a target and press Generate. Every sample is decoded, its (use …) preamble rebuilt, then parsed and validated — nothing is filtered silently, and rejected documents are shown with the parser's reason.

What this is not. Validity here means well-formed, not feasible. The validator checks the structure of a process, not its physics or its chemistry: a document can parse, validate, and still be wrong. No procedure shown here has ever been run. Recovering the reactants a patent used is a proxy, not a proof.
